## Citeable factual product summary

MyBingoCard is a browser-based bingo card maker for custom printable cards and online bingo games. A visitor can begin creating without an account, add their own words or generate ideas, change the grid and appearance, and print one individual PDF card free. An account is required to save a card for later access across devices. Use-case pages cover classrooms, events, team activities, trivia, and other themed cards.

This summary must be reverified against live behavior before public reuse.

## Answer-first content modules

### What is the fastest way to make a custom bingo card?

1. Open the card maker.
2. Add your own words or generate ideas for the chosen theme.
3. Select the grid size and customize colors or styling.
4. Review the card and save it to an account if you want to reopen it later.
5. Print an individual card or use the available online-play workflow.

**Action:** Create a bingo card.

### Can I start without creating an account?

Yes. Visitors can begin editing a draft on the current device before signing in. An account is required when saving the card for later account access. Public wording must distinguish local draft persistence from account-backed saving.

### Is it free?

Starting the card and printing one individual PDF card are free. Bulk cards, subscriptions, exports, or other features must be described from their current live pricing and product behavior; do not generalize “free” beyond verified capabilities.

### What should I put on a bingo card?

Offer concise examples grouped by real task:

- Classroom vocabulary or review prompts
- Conference networking prompts
- Team-building observations
- Trivia answers or categories
- Wedding or event guest prompts
- Community fundraiser themes

Each answer should link to the most relevant guide and one direct create action, not a chain of generic generator pages.

## Success and failure thresholds

**Scale:** at least 10 ChatGPT pageviews in a comparable window, 30% `/create` reach, 40% edit rate among entrants, and at least one first-card creator within 30 days.

**Iterate:** traffic recovers but `/create` reach is 15–29%, or the sample is under ten sessions.

**Stop/reposition:** after 30 days and confirmed crawlability, no traffic increase, or increased traffic has less than 15% `/create` reach and less than 25% meaningful-edit rate.
